# Awesome-Food-Delivery-Management

# Top Food Delivery Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Multi-Channel Ordering, POS Integration, Delivery Aggregation, Dispatch & Restaurant Online Ordering*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Food Delivery Management**. These systems help restaurants connect to delivery marketplaces, manage first-party online ordering, sync menus and orders to POS, and handle dispatch and kitchen operations.

**Examples** include Deliverect, Otter, UrbanPiper, Flipdish, Chowly, Checkmate / ItsaCheckmate, Restoke, Deliverect Dispatch, and Orders.co (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Multi-aggregator delivery management is mostly commercial. Strong open options exist for first-party online ordering and restaurant management (**TastyIgniter**, **NextOrders**, and related projects). This section lists the best available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap for full marketplace integration.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Deliverect](https://www.deliverect.com/)**  
  Leading delivery management platform that integrates restaurants with multiple delivery channels, POS systems, and provides order routing, menu sync, and dispatch capabilities.

- **[Otter](https://www.tryotter.com/)**  
  Restaurant technology platform for multi-channel ordering, kitchen display, delivery management, and operational tools for restaurants and ghost kitchens.

- **[UrbanPiper](https://www.urbanpiper.com/)**  
  Order aggregation and restaurant technology platform focused on connecting POS, delivery apps, and online ordering across markets.

- **[Flipdish](https://www.flipdish.com/)**  
  Online ordering and restaurant management platform for first-party ordering, branded apps, and takeaway/delivery operations.

- **[Chowly](https://www.chowly.com/)**  
  Online ordering integration platform that connects restaurants to third-party delivery marketplaces and POS systems.

- **[Checkmate / ItsaCheckmate](https://www.itsacheckmate.com/)**  
  Order management and integration platform for multi-channel restaurant ordering and POS connectivity.

- **[Restoke](https://www.restoke.io/)**  
  Restaurant operations and ordering platform supporting multi-location and delivery workflows.

- **[Deliverect Dispatch](https://www.deliverect.com/)**  
  Dispatch and last-mile coordination capabilities within the Deliverect ecosystem for delivery operations.

- **[Orders.co and similar first-party ordering platforms](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Additional platforms focused on branded online ordering, commissions reduction, and direct customer relationships.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[TastyIgniter](https://github.com/tastyigniter/TastyIgniter)**  
  Powerful open-source online ordering, table reservation, and restaurant management system (Laravel-based, MIT)—menus, orders, multi-location, and customer-facing storefronts.

- **[NextOrders / food](https://github.com/nextorders)**  
  Self-hosted open-source food delivery website focused on first-party ordering with zero marketplace commissions—branded storefront and typed schema for backend integration.

- **[WebResto / RestoCore](https://github.com/webresto)**  
  Open-source food delivery and restaurant e-commerce core and related applications for building delivery websites.

- **[Odoo / ERPNext restaurant and POS modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Open ERP modules for restaurant operations, POS, and basic online ordering that can be extended for delivery workflows.

- **[Open POS and kitchen-display projects](https://github.com/)**  
  Community POS, KDS, and order-ticket tools usable in restaurant kitchens.

- **[Menu and catalog open APIs](https://github.com/)**

- **[Delivery routing and dispatch open tools](https://github.com/)**

- **[Self-hosted ordering front-ends](https://github.com/)**  
  Open storefronts and progressive web apps for restaurants wanting direct online ordering.

- **[Integration adapter open examples](https://github.com/)**

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **TastyIgniter** or **NextOrders** for first-party online ordering and branded customer experience without marketplace commissions.
- Extending **Odoo/ERPNext** when the restaurant already runs open ERP for inventory and accounting.
- Accepting that deep, reliable integrations with major delivery marketplaces (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo, etc.), multi-POS support, and enterprise multi-location orchestration still favor commercial platforms (Deliverect, Otter, UrbanPiper, Chowly, Flipdish, Checkmate, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on direct customer relationships, data ownership, and lower fees for independent restaurants.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Run a self-hosted ordering site (TastyIgniter / NextOrders) for first-party orders → print or push to POS/KDS → optionally use a commercial aggregator platform for third-party marketplace channels → manage dispatch with open or commercial tools. Suitable for independent restaurants and groups that want to own the customer relationship. Chains and high-volume multi-channel operators typically rely on commercial delivery management platforms.
