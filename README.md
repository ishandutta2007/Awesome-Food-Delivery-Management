## 📊 Market Insights & Industry Overview

> 📈 **Market Size & Structure**: The global food delivery management software market was valued at approximately **$4.8 Billion in 2025** and is projected to reach **$11.5 Billion by 2032** (CAGR ~13.2%).
> 
> 🧩 **Market Fragmentation**: The sector is **moderately fragmented**, featuring high-valuation category leaders (like Deliverect and Otter) alongside market-specific aggregators (e.g., UrbanPiper in Asia/ME) and niche D2C ordering platforms (Flipdish, Chowly). While enterprise chains gravitate toward top commercial hubs for deep multi-marketplace POS APIs, independent operators increasingly adopt self-hosted open-source software to eliminate commission overheads.

## 💡 Architectural Guidance

When architecting a modern food delivery tech stack:
1. **D2C First-Party Orders**: Utilize self-hosted open-source software like **TastyIgniter** or **NextOrders** to capture direct orders without paying marketplace commissions (typically 15-30%).
2. **Third-Party Marketplace Aggregation**: For Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo, or Grubhub, integrate a commercial middleware (e.g., **Deliverect**, **Otter**, **UrbanPiper**) to inject orders straight into your POS and Kitchen Display System (KDS).
3. **Dispatch & Last-Mile**: Use open routing tools or specialized modules like **Deliverect Dispatch** to manage in-house delivery drivers.
